Step by step

How to approach your FIFO application FIFO 申請四步驟

Landing a mining camp role is more straightforward than most people assume, provided you take the right steps in the right order. 進入礦區工作比大多數人想像的更有跡可循，只要依照正確順序，一步一步來。

1

Search the right keyword on Seek在 Seek 輸入正確關鍵字

Head straight to seek.com.au and type "FIFO Utility" into the search bar. This is a broad, high-demand role category — precisely the entry point you want as a newcomer. Avoid narrowing the search too early; casting a wider net at this stage works in your favour.直接前往 seek.com.au，在搜尋欄輸入 「FIFO Utility」。這是一個需求量大且門檻相對友善的職位類型，非常適合作為新手的切入點。不要太早縮小搜尋範圍，廣撒網在這個階段對你有利。

2

Read every job listing carefully認真閱讀每一則招聘廣告

Resist the urge to mass-apply without reading. Go through each listing, whether posted directly by a company or through a recruitment agency, and note the precise qualifications required. Understanding the criteria before you apply means your resume reaches the shortlist instead of the bin.不要急著亂投。無論是直接雇主還是人力仲介 Agency，都要仔細讀完每則廣告的要求。在投遞前先理解條件，才能確保你的履歷不會第一關就被刷掉。

3

Obtain your essential certifications備齊必要證照

Think of these as your entry ticket. Without them, your application is almost certain to be filtered out before a hiring manager ever sees it. See the full breakdown below, including costs, where to enrol, and which to prioritise first.把這些證照想成你的「入場券」。沒有它們，你的申請幾乎在人資看到之前就會被過濾掉。下方有完整的證照清單，包含費用、哪裡可以報名，以及哪張要優先取得。

4

Make your resume work as hard as you do讓你的履歷和你一樣努力

Your certifications are your hard skills, and your resume is the packaging. Recruiters process hundreds of applications daily, and a poorly formatted resume gets rejected within seconds. A clean, Australia-standard layout ensures that all the effort you've invested in obtaining your licences is actually seen.證照是你的硬實力，履歷是你的軟實力。人資每天要處理幾百份申請，格式混亂的履歷幾秒內就會被淘汰。一份清晰、符合澳洲規格的履歷，才能確保你努力考來的證照真正被看見。

FAQ

What you actually want to know礦區常見問答

The questions that come up again and again, answered plainly.那些大家最常問的問題。

1What does a FIFO roster actually look like?FIFO 的班表長什麼樣子？

The most common arrangements are 2:1 (two weeks on, one week home), 1:1 (one week on, one week off), and 8:6 (eight days on, six days off). Standard shifts run to 12 hours per day. Night shifts are common and attract an additional allowance. Longer rosters such as 3:1 or 4:1 suit people who prefer intensive work blocks followed by extended time off.班表因公司和職位而不同，最常見的有 2:1（工作 2 週，休 1 週）、1:1（工作 1 週，休 1 週）和 8:6（工作 8 天，休 6 天）。每日工時通常是 12 小時，有時會輪到夜班，但通常有額外的夜班津貼。

2Does the company cover flights, meals, and accommodation?機票、住宿、三餐都包嗎？

Yes. While you are on your swing, the employer covers everything: charter flights (from Perth or Brisbane), on-site accommodation, and full meals through the camp mess hall. Breakfast and dinner are buffet-style; lunch is a packed crib from supplied ingredients. Because your living expenses drop to near-zero on swing, FIFO is one of the most efficient ways to build savings.是的！只要在班期間（Swing），公司通常全包：包機票（從伯斯或布里斯本出發）、包住（有冷氣和 Wi-Fi 的單人或雙人房）、包吃（早晚自助吧，中午提供材料讓你打包 Crib 便當）。在礦區幾乎花不到什麼錢，這也是 FIFO 存錢效率極高的原因。

3Can I apply with no prior mining experience?完全沒有礦區經驗，可以申請嗎？

Absolutely. Entry-level roles are designed for career changers and first-timers. Hiring managers prioritise a positive attitude, strong safety awareness, physical fitness, the required certifications, and a clean resume that meets Australian formatting standards.完全可以！Utility、Trade Assistant、Offsider 這些入門職位本來就是為轉職者和新手設計的。礦區人資更看重積極態度、安全意識、體能狀況，以及備齊基本證照和符合澳洲規格的履歷。

4What are the on-site living conditions like?礦區的住宿環境怎麼樣？

Modern mining camps are considerably more comfortable than most people expect. Accommodation is typically in single or shared rooms with ensuite facilities, air conditioning, and Wi-Fi. Most sites have a gym, recreational area, and a licensed bar. Think of it as a self-contained remote village rather than roughing it in the outback.現代礦區營地比很多人想像的舒適很多。通常是附獨立衛浴的單人或雙人房，有冷氣和 Wi-Fi，還設有健身房、休閒區和酒吧。把它想像成一個自給自足的小型社區，而不是在荒野露營。

5How do salaries work across different levels?入門職位和技術職位的薪資差多少？
  • Entry level (Utility, TA, Offsider): $35–$50/hr. Where rosters are stable and hours sufficient, total monthly earnings can be considerably higher with night shift allowances included.入門職位（Utility、TA、Offsider）：時薪約 $35–$50 AUD。班表穩定、工時足夠的情況下，加計夜班津貼後每月收入相當可觀。
  • Skilled trades (Chef, Mechanic, Electrician): $40–$80+/hr. Australian-licensed tradespeople are consistently among the best-compensated workers on site.技術職位（廚師、Mechanic、電工）：時薪從 $40 起，高的可到 $80+ AUD。持有澳洲認可執照的技術藍領，是礦區薪資最高的一群。

Note — Rates referenced from Casual listings on Seek.com.au, for general estimation purposes only. Actual remuneration may vary based on prior work experience, English proficiency, interview outcome, and employer policy. Please refer to the employment contract provided by the mining company or recruitment agency upon a successful offer. 備註｜以上時薪以 Seek.com.au 所列 Casual 職缺為參考，僅供求職前初步評估之用。實際薪資可能因過往工作經驗、英文能力、面試結果及雇主政策而有所不同，請以礦區公司或仲介提供的工作合約內容為準。
