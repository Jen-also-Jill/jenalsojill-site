FAQ

Jen Co 西澳伯斯咖啡課常見 QAFrequently asked questions

目前提供的咖啡課內容實際在上什麼？What do the classes actually cover?

不是看老師示範、你在旁邊拍照的那種課。整堂課你會一直站在機器前面自己做。

從認識濃縮咖啡的萃取標準開始，接著花大量時間練習打奶泡 —— 用手感受鋼杯的溫度，知道幾度該停。然後實際拉出圖案。

依照你選的課程，會做到不同程度：

  • Entry Level（70 分鐘）：Flat white、Latte、Cappuccino，基礎奶泡與愛心拉花
  • Latte Art（90 分鐘）：同上，追加拉花(推心 Tulip)
  • Advanced（130 分鐘）：澳洲咖啡店常見的六種飲品，進階拉花技巧，另附求職咖啡師電子書

每一堂都含實體講義和完課證書護貝版，回家之後還能照著練。

This isn’t a class where you watch and take photos. You’re standing at the machine making coffee the whole time.

We start with what a good espresso extraction looks like, then spend most of the session on milk — feeling the jug with your hand so you know when to stop. Then you pour.

How far you get depends on the class:

  • Entry (70 min): Flat White, Latte, Cappuccino, basic milk and a heart
  • Latte Art (90 min): the above plus tulips
  • Advanced (130 min): the six drinks Australian cafes serve daily, advanced latte art, plus a barista job-hunting e-book

Every class comes with printed notes and a laminated certificate, so you can keep practising at home.

英文不好或中文不好，會影響上課嗎？Will it be a problem if my English or Mandarin isn’t strong?

不會。中英雙語授課，可以選需要的語言模式上課。

而且 1 on 1 不用擔心聽不懂又不好意思打斷，想問幾次都可以，時間非常充裕。

很多來上課的人就是因為在英文團課吸收有限，後來又追加報名 1 on 1 咖啡課程。

No. Classes are taught in both English and Mandarin — whichever you’re comfortable with.

And in a 1 on 1 it’s just you. No need to worry about missing something and feeling awkward about interrupting. Ask as many times as you like; nobody is waiting on you.

A lot of people book precisely because they didn’t feel comfortable asking questions over and over in an English-speaking workplace.

對咖啡 0 概念、完全不喝咖啡，也能上嗎？I know nothing about coffee and I don’t even drink it. Can I still come?

可以。Entry Level 基礎咖啡課是為零基礎設計的，不需要任何先備知識。

不喝咖啡也完全沒問題 —— 做咖啡跟喝咖啡是兩件事。課堂上會教你怎麼用眼睛觀察的、用耳朵聽、用手去判斷奶泡和溫度。實際上也有學員是上課單純想學一項技能而來的。

Yes. Entry is built for people starting from zero — no prior knowledge needed.

Not drinking coffee is fine too; making it and drinking it are different things. You’ll learn to judge by sight, sound and touch rather than taste. Some students come purely because they need it for work, or just want a new skill.

在台灣（其他亞洲國家) 學過咖啡，還建議要報名上課嗎？I’ve learnt coffee back home. Is this still worth booking?

依照每個人的需求。如果是未來想在澳洲咖啡店工作，上課會幫助了解澳洲咖啡文化。

澳洲咖啡店的飲品品項跟亞洲國家不太一樣 —— Flat white、Long Mac、Short Mac、Piccolo 這些在亞洲可能少見，但在澳洲這裡是常見要會做的基本款。份量、奶泡厚度、出杯節奏也都有當地的標準。

有基礎的人我通常會建議直接上 Advanced，把澳洲常見飲品一次補齊，拉花也練到穩定的程度。如果你本來就會拉花，那麼上課內容可以調成加強求職面試技巧。

Depends what you’re after. If you want to work in an Australian cafe, the gap is bigger than you’d think.

The menu here is different — Flat White, Long Mac, Short Mac and Piccolo are everyday orders in Australia but rare elsewhere. Volumes, foam texture and the pace behind the bar all follow local standards.

If you already have a base, Advanced is usually the better fit: you cover all six Australian staples in one session and get your latte art to a level you can use on shift.
